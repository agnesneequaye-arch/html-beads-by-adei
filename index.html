Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  1. Q: How long does it take to complete a custom order?
    A: Standard catalog pieces ship within 2–4 business days. Custom orders and full bridal sets take 5–10 business days depending on design complexity.
  2. Q: Do you ship internationally?
    A: Yes! We ship nationwide within Ghana and offer international shipping worldwide.
  3. Q: Are the bags durable?
    A: Absolutely. All our beaded bags are woven using high-strength fishing lines and durable frame techniques to ensure they carry your essentials securely.
