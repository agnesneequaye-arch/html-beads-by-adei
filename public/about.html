About Us

At Beads by Adei, we believe accessories should tell a story. Based in Ghana, our brand celebrates rich cultural heritage through modern, handcrafted designs. Every bead is carefully chosen, strung, and woven to create timeless pieces that make you stand out at any event—whether it is a grand celebration, a traditional wedding, or a chic night out.
